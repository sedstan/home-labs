# Kindle Fire 8 (7th Generation) – Factory Reset Procedure

## 🧩 Device Overview

- **Model:** Kindle Fire 8 (7th Gen)
- **OS:** Fire OS 5.6.4.0 (based on Android 5.1.1)
- **Use Case:** Common budget tablet often used by customers for personal or family content consumption.
- **Support Relevance:** IT technicians may be asked to troubleshoot or reset these devices when users are locked out, experiencing performance issues, or preparing to sell/gift the device.

---

## 🎯 Scenario

> *Customer has forgotten their PIN and cannot access their Kindle Fire 8 (7th Gen). They request a full factory reset so they can reconfigure the device from scratch.*

---

## 🛠️ Reset Procedure

### Method: **Hard Reset via Recovery Mode**

1. **Power Off Device:**
   - Hold the Power button for ~20 seconds to ensure it fully shuts down.

2. **Enter Recovery Mode:**
   - Hold **Power + Volume Down** simultaneously.
   - When the **Amazon logo** appears, release **Power** but **keep holding Volume Down**.
   - Wait for the **Android Recovery menu** to appear.

3. **Navigate Recovery Menu:**
   - Use **Volume buttons** to highlight: `Wipe data/factory reset`
   - Press **Power** to select.

4. **Confirm Reset:**
   - Highlight: `Yes -- delete all user data`
   - Press **Power** to confirm.

5. **Reboot Device:**
   - After wipe completes, select `Reboot system now`.

---

## 🔐 Post-Reset Behaviour

- Device reboots into setup screen.
- User must **sign in with the original Amazon account** used before the reset.
  - This is part of Amazon’s account-based anti-theft measure (similar to FRP on Android).

---

## 🧠 Lessons Learned

- **Volume button combinations** differ across Kindle generations — always verify the correct combo for each model.
- **Amazon account lock-in** is strict. Factory reset does not remove ownership association.
- Good opportunity to explain account recovery steps to the customer.
- Recovery Mode UI resembles stock Android recovery — helps with pattern recognition across devices.
